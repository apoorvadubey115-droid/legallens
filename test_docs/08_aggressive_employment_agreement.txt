EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT & INVENTIONS AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between Apex Global Enterprises ("Employer") and the employee ("Employee").

SECTION 1: OVERBROAD INVENTIONS ASSIGNMENT
Employee agrees that all ideas, inventions, patent applications, artistic works, software repositories, mobile applications, and concepts developed, written, or conceived by Employee during the entire term of employment—whether developed on company time or personal time, using personal equipment, during evenings or weekends, and regardless of whether related to Employer's line of business—shall immediately become the sole and exclusive property of Employer without additional compensation.

SECTION 2: FIVE-YEAR RESTRICTIVE COVENANT & EMPLOYMENT LOCKOUT
Employee agrees that for a period of five (5) years following the termination of employment for any reason, Employee shall not directly or indirectly work for, consult for, advise, invest in, or serve on the board of any enterprise engaged in technology, marketing, financial services, or software anywhere in North America.

SECTION 3: MANDATORY LIQUIDATED DAMAGES ON RESIGNATION
If Employee resigns employment prior to completing twenty-four (24) months of service, Employee shall pay Employer a mandatory liquidated training and turnover fee of $45,000 within fourteen (14) days of departure.

SECTION 4: UNILATERAL WAGE DEDUCTIONS
Employer reserves the unrestricted right to deduct from Employee's wages, accrued vacation pay, or final salary check any amounts Employer deems necessary to offset potential client losses, hardware replacement estimates, or administrative audit overhead, without requiring prior judicial determination.

SECTION 5: ARBITRATION WAIVER & AT-WILL TERMINATION
Employer may terminate Employee at any time without cause or notice. Any claims arising from termination must be arbitrated under rules chosen solely by Employer, with Employee bearing all initial filing and arbitrator daily fees.
