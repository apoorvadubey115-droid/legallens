COMMERCIAL OFFICE SPACE LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease ("Lease") is made between MetroTower Commercial Holdings ("Landlord") and the tenant entity ("Tenant").

SECTION 1: UNRESTRICTED PASS-THROUGH OF STRUCTURAL CAPITAL REPAIRS
Tenant shall pay as Additional Rent its proportionate share of all Operating Expenses. Operating Expenses shall include, without limitation or cap, all foundation replacements, exterior building structural repairs, asbestos remediation, and roofing overhauls, regardless of whether said defects existed prior to Tenant's occupancy or outlive the term of this Lease.

SECTION 2: MANDATORY SECURITY DEPOSIT FORFEITURE
The security deposit of $30,000 shall be held by Landlord. Tenant acknowledges that any notice of late payment, regardless of whether subsequently cured within statutory grace periods, shall cause immediate and permanent forfeiture of the entire security deposit as liquidated administrative fees.

SECTION 3: UNILATERAL FORCED RELOCATION RIGHT
Landlord reserves the unrestricted right at any time upon forty-eight (48) hours' written notice to relocate Tenant to another unit within Landlord's regional portfolio. Tenant shall bear 100% of all moving, cabling, construction, and business interruption expenses resulting from such relocation.

SECTION 4: CONFESSION OF JUDGMENT & EVICTION NOTICE WAIVER
Tenant hereby irrevocably authorizes any attorney appointed by Landlord to appear in any court of record and confess judgment against Tenant for all unpaid rent, projected future rent through lease expiration, and Landlord's attorney fees. Tenant expressly waives all rights to prior notice, jury trial, summary proceeding defense, and right of appeal.

SECTION 5: BLANKET INDEMNIFICATION FOR LANDLORD CASUALTY
Tenant agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Landlord against all claims, injuries, property losses, or building fires occurring on the Premises, even if caused directly by Landlord's defective electrical wiring or gross maintenance negligence.
